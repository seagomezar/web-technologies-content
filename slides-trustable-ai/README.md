# Workshop Deck: Building Trustable AI at 100 MPH (DevFest Pereira 2026)

English · [Español](./README.es.md)

## Overview

Complete **37-slide** interactive facilitator presentation (90-minute plan) styled in the official **Web @DevFest Pereira 2026** visual theme to lead all 12 steps of Google's official [**Building Trustable AI at 100 MPH**](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/trustable-at-100-mph?hl=en) Codelab, co-authored by **Sebastián Gómez**, Krupa Galiya, Todd Kerpelman, and Ocupop.

> **Updated Official Workshop Repository:** [**https://github.com/seagomezar/trustable-ai-codelab**](https://github.com/seagomezar/trustable-ai-codelab) *(highlighted directly on Slide 2 to replace the offline upstream Codelab link).*

Architecturally, this package is a zero-bundler, offline-capable HTML/JavaScript slide deck (`index.html`, `deck.js`), synchronized dual-window facilitator console (`presenter.html`, `presenter.js`), bilingual slide dataset (`workshop-en.js`, `workshop-es.js`, `workshop.js`, `i18n.js`), and interactive 10 Hz Split-Brain telemetry cockpit simulator (`live-sim.html`), governed by explicit, inspectable finite-state machines for planned clock timing, presenter connection status, ambient lane animations, directional view transitions, and cockpit simulation regions.

### Key Entry Points

- [`index.html?lang=en`](./index.html) — Main 37-slide workshop presentation (works over local HTTP and directly via offline `file://` URLs; defaults to Spanish unless `?lang=en`).
- [`presenter.html?lang=en`](./presenter.html) — Synchronized Facilitator / Presenter console with a 90-minute timer, current & next slide previews, and per-step speaker scripts (press **`P`** inside the deck).
- [`live-sim.html?lang=en`](./live-sim.html) — Interactive Split-Brain cockpit simulator embedded on Slide 18 and runnable standalone (5 coach personas, `0.6g` rain toggle, Physics ON/OFF, live Friction Circle).
- [`../workshops/trustable-ai-codelab/`](../workshops/trustable-ai-codelab/README.md) & [`github.com/seagomezar/trustable-ai-codelab`](https://github.com/seagomezar/trustable-ai-codelab) — Repaired, tested, and 2026-modernized workshop codebase (`streaming-telemetry-server` FastAPI SSE + `koru-application` React/Vite + `@google/genai ^3.3.0`).

### Keyboard & Pointer Controls

- **`→` / `Space` / `PageDown` / `Enter`:** advance code focus / build step, then next slide (`↓` also advances; `Home` / `End` jump to first / last slide).
- **`←` / `PageUp` / `Backspace`:** previous step or slide (`↑` also steps back).
- **`Shift + →` / `Shift + ←`:** jump across whole slides.
- **`P`:** open synchronized Presenter View.
- **`F`:** toggle fullscreen.
- **`C`:** hide or show bottom control bar.
- **Click / Swipe:** click `#viewport` background or single-finger horizontal swipe (`|dx| > 45`, `|dx| > |dy| * 1.4`) to advance/rewind without intercepting interactive controls or `.sim-frame-wrap`.
- **Presenter Remote Keys (`presenter.js`):** `ArrowRight` / `PageDown` / `Space` (`next`), `ArrowLeft` / `PageUp` (`previous`).

### Customizing City or Duration

```text
slides-trustable-ai/index.html?lang=en&city=Pereira%202026&minutes=90
```

## Invariants

- **Deferred Script Execution Order**: Both `index.html` and `presenter.html` load `<script defer>` tags strictly in sequence (`i18n.js` -> `workshop-es.js` -> `workshop-en.js` -> `workshop.js` -> `deck.js` / `presenter.js`) so `window.I18N` and `window.TALK` are populated before consumer IIFEs execute `[index.html:script]` `[presenter.html:script]`.
- **Locale Resolution & Same-Origin Rewriting**: Locale resolves to `'en'` if and only if `?lang=en` (defaulting to `'es'`), and `I18N.url()` wraps `new URL(href, location.href)` in `try/catch` (returning `href` unchanged on malformed inputs) and mutates URLs only when `target.origin === location.origin` and `/\.html$|\/$/.test(target.pathname)` `[i18n.js:lang]` `[i18n.js:url]` `[workshop.js:window.TALK]`.
- **Bilingual 37-Slide & 90-Minute Parity**: `window.WORKSHOP_EN.slides` and `window.WORKSHOP_ES.slides` both contain exactly 37 slide objects whose `minutes` values are positive integers summing to `90` (`plannedMinutes === 90`), matching the 4 roadmap phases (`18 + 24 + 26 + 22 = 90`) `[workshop-en.js:window.WORKSHOP_EN.slides]` `[workshop-es.js:window.WORKSHOP_ES.slides]`.
- **Build-Step & Facilitator-Note Alignment**: Every slide satisfies `slide.notes.length === slide.builds.length + 1`, and every `'code'` / `'terminal'` slide satisfies `codeFocus.length === stepCallouts.length === builds.length + 1` with 1-based line indices bounded by `slide.code` line count `[workshop-en.js:window.WORKSHOP_EN.slides]` `[workshop-es.js:window.WORKSHOP_ES.slides]`.
- **Explicit Clock, Connection, Animation, & Cockpit Machines**: `CLOCK_TRANSITIONS` deterministically governs `'idle' -> 'running' <-> 'paused'` with derived `overrun` (`planned > 0 && elapsed >= planned * 60`) while keeping `{ startedAt, pausedAt, pausedMs }` wire-compatible `[deck.js:transitionClock]` `[presenter.js:deriveClock]`; `CONNECTION_TRANSITIONS` transitions `'connecting' -> 'connected' | 'standalone'` and recovers `'standalone' -> 'connected'` whenever `'deck-state'` arrives `[presenter.js:syncConnection]`; `ANIMATION_TRANSITIONS` governs `'inactive' | 'playing' | 'paused_user' | 'paused_hidden' | 'reduced_motion'` on `'splitbrain'` (`step === 0`) so manual build steps (`step > 0`) pin their lane cleanly (`'inactive'`) and tab switching never resumes `'paused_user'` `[deck.js:sendAnimation]`; `COCKPIT_TRANSITIONS` governs `playback` (`'playing' | 'paused'`), `surface` (`'dry' | 'wet'`), `guardrails` (`'enabled' | 'disabled'`), `persona`, and `sectorPhase` (`0..4`) with zero background intervals while paused or hidden `[live-sim.html:send]` `[live-sim.html:syncSimLoop]`.
- **Index Clamping, Input Guards, & State Isolation**: `parse()` and `navigate()` clamp `index` to `[0, slides.length - 1]` and `step` to `[0, slides[index].builds.length]`, while `deck.getState()`, `presenter.getState()`, and `cockpitSim.getState()` return fresh snapshot objects; `deck.js` ignores IME composition, `Alt`/`Ctrl`/`Meta` chords, `INPUT`/`TEXTAREA`/`SELECT` targets, `Enter`/`Space` on `a,button,summary`, and `#viewport` clicks on `a,button,input,select,textarea,iframe,.sim-frame-wrap` or text selections (handling `.flow-pause` before generic button guards), `presenter.js` ignores IME composition, `Alt`/`Ctrl`/`Meta` chords, and `input,textarea,select,button,summary,a,[contenteditable]` targets, and unrevealed `.pending` build items plus presenter `.preview` wrappers carry `inert` `[deck.js:parse]` `[deck.js:keydown]` `[presenter.js:keydown]`.
- **Command Deduplication & Origin Verification**: Inbound `deck-command` messages carrying `id` are deduplicated via `seenCommands` (FIFO-capped at 128 entries) `[deck.js:seenCommands]`, and `window` `message` listeners enforce `e.source` matching plus `e.origin === location.origin` whenever `location.origin !== 'null'` (preserving offline `file://` transport) `[deck.js:receive]` `[presenter.js:receive]`.
- **Preview Channel Isolation, Reduced Motion, & Background Throttling**: Presenter preview iframes override `session` to `${session}-preview-${frame.id}` so embedded decks never pollute the live session channel `[presenter.js:preview]`; `document.startViewTransition` (with directional `types: ['forward' | 'backward' | 'step']` and graceful fallback) and the 1500 ms `'splitbrain'` lane cycle respect `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` (with `.flow-pause` providing WCAG 2.2.2 Pause/Play control on `step === 0`), `live-sim.html` initializes `playback` to `'paused'` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` while keeping `#btn-pause` and immediate gauge updates interactive, and recurring intervals suspend while `document.hidden` is `true` `[deck.js:navigate]` `[deck.js:sendAnimation]` `[presenter.js:tick]` `[live-sim.html:syncSimLoop]`.
- **Offline Self-Containment & Bounded Radio Feed**: All fonts, stylesheets, images, and scripts resolve locally with zero external runtime network requests (`slide.code` snippets are inert display strings) `[workshop-en.js:window.WORKSHOP_EN.slides]` `[workshop-es.js:window.WORKSHOP_ES.slides]`, and `#feed` in `live-sim.html` builds messages via DOM text nodes (no `innerHTML`) and caps visible radio messages at 4 DOM nodes across 5 contiguous track phases covering `[0.00, 1.00]` `[live-sim.html:pushMsg]` `[live-sim.html:phases]`.

- **History API Wrapping, Directional Transitions, & Focus Routing**: Wraps `history.pushState` and `history.replaceState` to synchronize injected `<nav class="language-switch">` links (`aria-current="page"` on the active locale) `[i18n.js:DOMContentLoaded]`, pushes `#<index+1>.<step>` URL hashes via `history.pushState`, tracks `'idle' | 'transitioning'` via `sendTransition`, triggers directional `document.startViewTransition`, and routes programmatic focus to `<article class="slide" tabindex="-1">` `[deck.js:navigate]` `[deck.js:sendTransition]`.
- **Deck, Presenter, & Simulator DOM & State Attribute Rendering**: `deck.js` sets `--scale` on `document.documentElement`, updates `--slide-bg`, `.embedded-preview`, `.hide-controls`, `.flow-paused`, and `data-clock-state` / `data-clock-overrun` / `data-animation-state` / `data-transition-state` on `document.body`, localizes shell controls for English and injects `#slide-select` and polite `role="status"` `#step-status` into `.controlbar`, rewrites `#viewport.innerHTML` (mounting `<iframe class="sim-frame" src="live-sim.html?lang=...">` on `'simulator'` slides), and updates `document.title` and `#progress` width `[deck.js:scale]` `[deck.js:paint]`; `presenter.js` reflects `data-connection-state` on `#connection` and `document.body`, reflects `data-clock-state` and `data-clock-overrun` on `.clock` (plus `data-clock-state` on `document.body`), relabels UI headings/titles for English, and renders `#script`, `#plan`, `#schedule`, `#sources`, and `#next-*` `[presenter.js:syncConnection]` `[presenter.js:render]` `[presenter.js:tick]`; `live-sim.html` reflects `data-playback-state`, `data-surface-state`, `data-guardrail-state`, `data-persona`, and `data-sector-phase` on `document.body`, syncs `[aria-pressed]` persona toggles, drives `--level` CSS custom properties for compositor `scaleY()` pedal bars, and seeds `#feed` at startup via `emitForPhase(1)` and `emitForPhase(2)` `[live-sim.html:syncDom]` `[live-sim.html:emitForPhase]`.
- **Cross-Window Broadcasts, Popups & Fullscreen**: Opens popup window `'sg-trustable-ai-presenter'` (`1380x900`) via `window.open` `[deck.js:openPresenter]`, exchanges sync messages over `BroadcastChannel` and `postMessage` `[deck.js:announce]` `[presenter.js:send]`, and invokes `requestFullscreen()` / `exitFullscreen()` with `webkit` fallbacks and `#fullscreen[aria-pressed]` synchronization `[deck.js:toggleFullscreen]`.
- **Background Interval & Timeout Timers**: Schedules a `1500 ms` `setInterval` cycling `.sb-lane.active` strictly while `sbAnim.state === 'playing'` `[deck.js:startAnim]`, a visibility-gated `500 ms` `setInterval` updating `#wall-clock`, `#elapsed`, `#remaining`, and `.clock.overrun` plus a cancellable `3000 ms` `connectTimer` in Presenter View `[presenter.js:tick]` `[presenter.js:connectTimer]`, and a `100 ms` `setInterval` running strictly when `cockpit.playback === 'playing' && !document.hidden` in the simulator `[live-sim.html:syncSimLoop]`.
