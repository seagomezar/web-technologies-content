SLIDE 33
Half screen speaker
Presenter slides are a great way to engage your 
audience by showing the presenter on on the 
right half of the screen and graphics on the left.
When designing presenter slides make sure your 
graphics never cross the centerline. This will 
make sure there is enough space to fit both the 
presenter and the graphic.
If your content is too large to fit on a presenter 
slide or is better for the learner with more space, 
please use a full-screen slide instead.
